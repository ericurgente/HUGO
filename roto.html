03The system

To the eye, ROTO seems wild, aggressive and maybe even dangerous, alluding to greater themes of human nature and change. However, it aims to connect at the deepest emotional level

Every visual decision follows from the brand's emotional foundations. Primitive, grunge and urban themes translated into a cohesive identity through colour, texture and symbolism.

[ A ] Colour palette & materials
[ B ] Symbolism

Drawing from stone age art and historical eras, ROTO developed three core symbols, each etched, engraved or embedded across the brand.

[ 01 ]Dagger14th-century Rondel + Picasso's Bullfight III.Craftsmanship and defense.
[ 02 ]MouflonSardinian horns + Alpine Krampus mythology.Primal instinct.
[ 03 ]StelarKosmaj Partisan monument, Belgrade.Explosion as renewal.
[ C ] Logo

Rough, primal, versatile, designed to be taken apart and still be identified as ROTO.

04The campaign

"Instinct" is the first breath of the brand into the city. Its passport to a collective launch.

"Instinct" wants to be reminiscent of something we have felt and known all along." A three-phase strategy designed to make the brand a visible scar of the city. Barcelona would discover ROTO before ROTO explained itself.

[ Phase 01 ]ROTO doors

ROTO Doors placed in Barcelona's underground bars and clubs as gateways into the brand's universe, alongside products displayed in glass boxes over stone slates, fragile on the outside, symbolic within.

[ Phase 02 ]The Event

First gathering at Refugi Antiaeri, a civil war bunker at Plaza Tetuan; underground techno by 240km/h, visual projections by Unax Lafuente, fashion and sound melted into raw concrete.

[ Phase 03 ]The film

A fashion film bridging product with brand purpose. Showing that ROTO is an accessories brand at heart, but a community above all.
